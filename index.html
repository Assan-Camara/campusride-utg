WHY CAMPUSRIDE?

A better plan for the journey to class.

Getting to lectures on time can be difficult when transport is uncertain. CampusRide UTG presents a private transportation service concept focused on making student travel easier to organize.

The website helps students understand the proposed service, learn how to enquire, and contact the service provider for confirmed routes, prices, and schedules.

Important: Routes, fares, departure times, and availability must be confirmed directly before travelling. This student project website does not process bookings or payments.
